# VNX.SI

## Software you need. Built by people you can meet.

VNX.SI is a marketplace for AI-built products and the builders behind them. Find software that already fits your business, ask for a tailored version, work directly with a builder, or describe what you need when the catalogue does not have the answer.

[Explore VNX.SI →](https://vnx.si)

> **Current scope — October 2026:** VNX.SI currently focuses on builder profiles, product listings, catalogue discovery, Inquiry, and Post a request. The platform does not process payments; clients and builders agree final scope, price, licence, and delivery directly.

## Start with the outcome

You do not need to begin by choosing a framework or a development process. Begin with the work you want software to improve.

### Buy

If a product already fits, inspect its problem statement, features, demo, pricing tiers, licence, support policy, and builder. Send a `Buy` Inquiry when you want to ask a question or discuss next steps.

### Customize

If a product is close but needs changes, choose `Customize` when the builder offers it. Share the context, budget range, and deadline so the conversation starts with a concrete brief.

### Hire the builder

You can work with the person who built a product on a separate project. Review their profile, skills, portfolio, and availability, then send a `Hire` Inquiry.

### Build similar

A product can be a useful starting point for a different business or workflow. `Build Similar` lets you explain what you want to keep and what needs to change.

## A clearer route from idea to conversation

1. **Discover.** Search the catalogue by category, delivery model, starting price, language, or verification badge. Browse the builder directory by skill, language, country, and availability.
2. **Understand.** Read the product page: the problem it addresses, intended users, features, technology context, pricing, licence, customization, support, and builder.
3. **Ask.** Send an Inquiry with a message, budget range, and optional deadline. The conversation continues in the VNX.SI web inbox.
4. **Request.** If you still cannot find the right fit, post a private request. The VNX.SI team can invite up to five builders to respond with proposals.
5. **Choose a direction.** Review proposals in your account, select a builder, and continue the conversation through Inquiry.

Signed-out clients confirm their email before an Inquiry or request is opened for a builder to see. Builders do not receive a client’s email through these flows.

## For builders: turn useful work into a durable presence

AI can make building faster. VNX.SI gives what you build a place to be found, understood, and connected to the next conversation.

Create a profile with your skills, working languages, availability, and portfolio. Publish a product with its problem, target users, features, demo, pricing, licence, customization options, and support policy. A product can represent a SaaS, source-code offering, or service package.

From there, the platform gives you several ways to work:

- receive Inquiries from people who want to buy, customize, hire, or build something similar;
- respond to private requests with an approach, price or price range, and expected timeline;
- show a catalogue of products instead of explaining the same work from scratch;
- use the AI tools that suit your process — VNX.SI is model agnostic.

During the founding phase, listings are intended to be free while the initial supply is built. [Become a builder →](https://vnx.si/for-builders)

## Trust you can read

VNX.SI uses evidence-based badges instead of star ratings it cannot substantiate. Each active badge appears with a verification date.

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Listed** | The VNX.SI team reviewed the listing for a clear description, pricing or a clear way to request pricing, and working links before publication. |
| **Demo verified** | The team opened the demo and saw it work over HTTPS at the time of review. A changed demo URL revokes the badge. |
| **In production** | The builder provided evidence of real customers using the product, and the team checked that evidence. |

These signals describe the checks they name. They do not replace your own review of a product’s fit, security, performance, licence, or commercial terms.

## A marketplace with a clear line on ranking

Organic ranking is never for sale. Product and builder lists cannot be moved by payment, partner commission, or monetization data. If VNX.SI introduces sponsored placement in a later phase, it will be a separate, clearly labelled area and will not change organic order.

The same separation guides partner work. A future partner link may be disclosed and measured through a controlled route, but a click is not treated as revenue and a commission cannot become a quality signal. Partnership conversations can start at [contact@vnx.si](mailto:contact@vnx.si).

## Built for more than one market

The interface supports English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Product and builder pages are designed to carry the context a client needs: what the product does, who it is for, how it is delivered, what it costs, and who can adapt it.

## What comes next

VNX.SI is building in stages. Later phases are planned to explore:

- AI Discovery that turns a plain-language idea into a structured brief;
- Buy / Customize / Build recommendations grounded in real products and builders;
- assisted builder matching and estimates, evaluated before automation;
- project, milestone, payment, payout, review, and maintenance workflows.

These are roadmap directions rather than current product promises. The first phase is about making supply discoverable and conversations useful enough to support the next one.
