## Rights boundary for these drafts

`LICENSE-CC0` describes a public-domain dedication and fallback licence for the work to which CC0 was applied. It also says that trademark and patent rights are not affected, provides the work as-is with disclaimers, and does not clear rights held by other people. The repository's licence statement is evidence of the upstream project's declared scope, not a separate clearance of every fact, name, example, or third-party item that may appear in a prompt.

The source-derived portions are recorded for provenance. VNX.SI adaptations add structure, examples, safety constraints, and output contracts. Those additions remain pending a VNX.SI licence decision:

- `license: null` means this draft does not grant a licence for the combined VNX.SI entry.
- `vnxsi_adaptation_license: null` means the adaptation is not released under CC0 by implication.
- The upstream CC0 field must not be read as a blanket licence for VNX.SI additions or third-party rights.
